## Internal Linking Rules

- Every article links to 2 related articles.
- Every article links to 1 tier page or build catalog page.
- Every cluster pillar links down to build-specific articles.
- Every build-specific article links back to the tier page and one broader pillar.

## Analytics Review

Monthly review:

- Top organic landing posts.
- Scroll depth by post.
- CTA clicks by post and CTA target.
- Newsletter submissions by post.
- Blog to signup and blog to plan visits.
- Posts with high traffic but low CTA clicks: rewrite intro and mid-article CTA.
